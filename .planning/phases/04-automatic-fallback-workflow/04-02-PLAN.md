<threat_model>
- Threat T-04-04: invalid target, late source shutdown, or selection-before-preheat
  violates heater/toolchanger safety. Mitigation: ready-time heater resolution, strict
  finite positive target validation, capture-before-immediate-shutdown, preheat with
  `wait=False` before physical selection, and no weakening of Klipper heater safety.
- Threat T-04-05: stage timeout or normal RESUME bypasses incomplete work. Mitigation:
  reactor deadline for async heating, synchronous adapter exception/post-return
  overrun checks, persistent blocked checkpoint, transactional RESUME capture, direct
  saved-handler delegation, and heating-timeout-only guarded continuation.
- Threat T-04-06: shared stage helpers accidentally apply unsafe automatic fallback
  rollback or break existing manual transition guarantees. Mitigation: explicit source
  policy, preserve manual rollback only where demonstrably safe, prohibit automatic
  rollback by API contract, and verify complete manual transition ordering separately.
- Selection and purge G-code are synchronous and cannot be safely preempted; this plan
  must not claim cancellation of arbitrary macros.
</threat_model>
