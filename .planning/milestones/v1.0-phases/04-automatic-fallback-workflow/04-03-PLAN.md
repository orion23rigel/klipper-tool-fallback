<threat_model>
- Threat T-04-07: orchestration publishes route truth too early, resumes an unowned or
  incomplete pause, or guesses which logical route failed. Mitigation: require known
  active logical ownership, persist failed state before stages, enforce locked ordering,
  rebuild mapping from current canonical state only after purge, and resume only after
  final persistence under explicit ownership.
- Threat T-04-08: failure after physical selection causes another candidate selection
  or rollback to the failed unloaded tool, compounding unsafe machine state. Mitigation:
  freeze candidate after preheat/selection begins, stop on every selection/purge
  exception or overrun, prohibit automatic physical rollback, preserve paused blocked
  checkpoint, and assert exactly one physical attempt.
- Threat: Phase 4 accidentally sends Phase 5 external notifications or claims hardware
  verification. Mitigation: local status/respond warnings only, no notify adapter calls,
  no example/integration changes, and explicit UAT deferral.
</threat_model>
